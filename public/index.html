The 561 Group

The 561 Group is a private organization for practical autonomy: research, engineering, publishing, public services, and economic coordination designed to help people and institutions keep agency through political, financial, technological, and ecological volatility.

We practice civic anarchism through explicit, bounded authority, voluntary participation, evidence, fair exchange, and interoperable tools rather than claims of statehood or legal sovereignty. Our aim is relational and economic sovereignty: the capacity to make choices, keep useful knowledge and infrastructure, recover from failure, and hand durable capabilities forward across generations.

That work includes long-horizon science, agent-native institutions, public-interest services, and enterprises built to remain useful as platforms and markets change. We value calm under volatility without confusing endurance with stagnation.

We also make room for play, craft, curiosity, and meaning. Durable institutions should help people live, not merely persist.
